why it exists

Every team needs the same warehouse to run, without a cloud bill

Give the warehouse-systems bounded-context estate a reproducible, cloud-free runtime: a kind cluster provisioned by a single `terraform apply` that carries every service, its data, its events and its observability with zero cloud dependencies. It owns the environment, not the workloads…

Today

Ten chart releases, a shared Postgres, Kafka, two gateways and a full observability plane run in one local kind cluster.

The catch

Everything is unauthenticated and loopback-only by design, and the shared Postgres and PgBouncer are single points of failure.

warehouse-systems · executive briefing · November 2026warehouse-infra
Give the warehouse-systems bounded-context estate a reproducible, cloud-free runtime: a kind cluster provisioned by a single `terraform apply` that carries every service, its data, its events and its observability with zero cloud dependencies. It owns the environment, not the workloads — each service repo keeps its own Helm chart, and this repo registers a context by adding one entry to `terraform/locals.tf`. It also owns fleet-level published facts (the process-path catalogue) that no single bounded context should own.
what it does

One command delivers the cluster, data, events and dashboards

One-command platform

One script creates the cluster, Postgres, Kafka, mesh, gateways and ArgoCD.

Two separate gateways

Kong serves APIs, Nginx serves screens, and a test keeps the two apart.

GitOps deployment

ArgoCD owns every chart release and heals drift on its own.

Shared data plane

One Postgres with a database and role per context, pooled, plus Kafka topics.

Observability plane

Traces, metrics and logs flow through one collector into Grafana.

Analytics enablement

Separate analytics databases and topics, with reports split from projectors.

competitive position

Against the market leaders, we cover part of the ground

PartialPlatform, Observability & Delivery vs Blue Yonder, Manhattan (zero-downtime CD, multi-AZ failover), SSI Schäfer (KPI telemetry)

Where we match or lead

  • Open, correlated telemetry: OTel Collector, Jaeger, Prometheus, Grafana, Loki + Alloy, Kiali, Kong metrics
  • Metric exemplars link to traces; log lines link to traces (two-way navigation)
  • Generated per-context Grafana dashboards: edge RED, business counters, Go runtime, logs
  • Harness engineering: mutation gates, architecture fitness tests, black-box e2e and soak

Gaps to close

  • High No alerting: Alertmanager disabled; no SLOs or error budgets
  • High Telemetry is ephemeral: Jaeger in-memory, Prometheus 24 h emptyDir, Loki no persistence
  • High Local kind cluster: no HA, multi-AZ failover or zero-downtime rollout evidence
  • Medium /healthz does not check the database

Leader capabilities are vendor-published; gaps reflect repo docs reviewed in November 2026.
